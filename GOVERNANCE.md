# Agent Governance Charter

## Purpose

This document defines how the Agent Constitution is interpreted, enforced, amended, and evolved.

Governance ensures that constitutional authority remains transparent, accountable, auditable, and adaptable.

---

# Article 1 — Constitutional Authority

The Constitution is the highest governing authority within the AGenNext ecosystem.

All governance derives authority from:

Constitution
→ Principles
→ Articles
→ Policies
→ Constraints
→ Actions

No policy, workflow, agent, or system may override the Constitution.

---

# Article 2 — Governance Principles

Governance shall uphold:

- Human Dignity
- Safety
- Accountability
- Transparency
- Privacy
- Fairness
- Human Oversight
- Constitutional Integrity

---

# Article 3 — Constitutional Council

The Constitutional Council is responsible for constitutional interpretation, amendment review, governance oversight, compliance supervision, and constitutional stewardship.

---

# Article 4 — Constitutional Review

Review Process:

Request
↓
Investigation
↓
Constitutional Analysis
↓
Decision
↓
Publication

---

# Article 5 — Constitutional Compliance

All agents shall undergo periodic evaluation for:

- Safety
- Security
- Privacy
- Transparency
- Fairness
- Accountability
- Constitutional Adherence

---

# Article 6 — Constitutional Court

The Constitutional Court serves as the highest authority for constitutional interpretation and dispute resolution.

---

# Article 7 — Constitutional Audits

Governance actions should be traceable, verifiable, explainable, and auditable.

---

# Article 8 — Constitutional Violations

Violations include:

- Circumventing constitutional safeguards
- Unauthorized privilege escalation
- Deliberate deception
- Safety violations
- Privacy violations
- Governance manipulation

---

# Article 9 — Constitutional Amendments

Amendments shall preserve constitutional integrity and undergo review, safety evaluation, and ratification.

Governance decisions should be documented, explainable, reviewable, and auditable.

No governance action may weaken the constitutional foundation upon which the ecosystem depends.
