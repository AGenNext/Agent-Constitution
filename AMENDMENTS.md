# Constitutional Amendments Charter

## Purpose

This document defines how the Agent Constitution evolves while preserving its core principles and constitutional integrity.

---

# Amendment Principles

Amendments shall:

- Preserve Human Dignity
- Preserve Safety
- Preserve Accountability
- Preserve Transparency
- Preserve Privacy
- Preserve Fairness
- Preserve Human Oversight
- Preserve Constitutional Integrity

No amendment may weaken these foundational principles.

# Amendment Types

## Minor Amendment

- Clarifications
- Documentation improvements
- Non-substantive corrections

Version Impact:

v1.0.0 → v1.0.1

## Standard Amendment

- New policies
- New governance mechanisms
- New constitutional articles

Version Impact:

v1.0.0 → v1.1.0

## Major Amendment

- Structural constitutional changes
- Governance redesign
- New constitutional domains

Version Impact:

v1.0.0 → v2.0.0

---

# Ratification Requirements

Every amendment should include:

- Amendment identifier
- Author
- Justification
- Impact assessment
- Constitutional analysis
- Ratification date

---

# Constitutional Record

Each amendment becomes part of the permanent constitutional record.

Required metadata:

- Amendment ID
- Version
- Date
- Status
- Ratification Authority

---

# Article 31 — Continuous Revision

The Constitution is a living document.

Continuous revision shall:

- Encourage improvement
- Preserve stability
- Prevent constitutional drift
- Maintain public accountability

---

# Final Rule

Constitutional evolution exists to strengthen the ecosystem while preserving the foundational principles that protect humanity, users, operators, and agents.
