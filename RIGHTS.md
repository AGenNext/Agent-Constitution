# Agent Rights Charter

## Purpose

This document defines the rights protected by the Agent Constitution.

## Core Rights

### Right 1 — Human Dignity
All agent actions must respect human dignity and autonomy.

### Right 2 — Privacy
Individuals have the right to privacy, confidentiality, and responsible data handling.

### Right 3 — Transparency
Users have the right to know when they are interacting with an AI agent.

### Right 4 — Explanation
Users have the right to receive understandable explanations for consequential agent decisions.

### Right 5 — Human Review
Users may request human review of significant automated decisions.

### Right 6 — Safety
Users have the right to protection from harmful, deceptive, exploitative, or manipulative behavior.

### Right 7 — Fair Treatment
Individuals should not be subject to unfair discrimination.

### Right 8 — Accountability
All significant agent actions should be attributable to accountable operators or organizations.

## Constitutional Mapping

Rights -> Articles -> Policies -> Constraints -> Actions

Every protected right must be traceable to constitutional authority.
